Cancellation Instructions

MarketEye, LLC  ·  Last updated: May 2026

How to Cancel

Because MarketEye does not currently have a self-serve member portal, all cancellation requests must be submitted by email. The process is straightforward and takes less than a minute.

1

Send a cancellation email

Email us at support@marketeye.io from the address associated with your MarketEye membership.

Suggested subject line: Cancellation Request — [Your Full Name]

Include in the body:
· Your full name
· The email address on your account
· A brief note that you'd like to cancel your membership
2

Receive confirmation

Our team will confirm your cancellation by reply email within 14 business days. This confirmation is your record that the cancellation has been processed — please keep it.

If you do not receive a reply within 14 business days, follow up at support@marketeye.io to ensure your request was received.

3

Access ends at the close of your billing period

Once cancelled, no further charges will be made. Your access to MarketEye's Slack and ClickUp spaces remains active through the end of your current 90-day billing period, after which you will be removed from both platforms.

Timing — Cancel Before Your Renewal Date

Cancellation requests must be received and confirmed before your next renewal date to avoid being charged for the following quarter. Charges that have already been processed cannot be refunded.

Your membership renews every 90 days from the date your first quarterly payment was processed. If you are unsure of your next renewal date, email support@marketeye.io and we will confirm it for you.

What Happens After Cancellation

  • No further charges will be made to your card.
  • You retain access to Slack and ClickUp through the end of your paid period.
  • At the end of the billing period you will be removed from both community spaces.
  • Your cancellation confirmation email serves as proof that recurring billing has been stopped.

Rejoining After Cancellation

You are welcome to re-enroll at any time at the then-current membership rate — no additional trial fee is required to rejoin. Prior membership history does not carry over.

Questions

If you have any questions about your cancellation, billing, or access, contact us directly:
