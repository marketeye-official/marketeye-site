What Is Trading? (Demystified)

Make Money in Any Market

More Than Just Buying Low

Most people think you can only be made when prices go up. But traders have a second weapon — the ability to profit from falling prices just as easily as rising ones.

This is called "going short." And it's one of the most powerful — and misunderstood — tools in any trader's arsenal. We'll demonstrate both live in the our free workshop using real stocks and real situations.

📈
Bull Market (Long Position)
Buy at a lower price, sell at a higher price. The profit equals the difference between your entry and exit price, adjusted for your position size.
📉
Bear Market (Short Position)
Sell high, buy back lower, keep the difference. The profit potential is equal whether the market goes up or down — your risk and reward are defined before you enter the trade.
Key Insight: The market itself doesn't matter. What matters is your ability to read its direction and position yourself correctly — whether that's up or down.
