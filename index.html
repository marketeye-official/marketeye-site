Frequently Asked

Questions Answered

What happens at the end of the workshop? ▼

At the end of the session, we introduce MarketEye — our mentorship program for people who want to take trading seriously. You'll learn how the program works, what the journey looks like, and how to apply. There's no obligation and no pitch. If it's a fit, you'll know.

How does the application process work? ▼

MarketEye isn't open enrollment. Every applicant goes through a short interview so we can understand where you're at and whether the program is the right fit for you. If you're accepted, you'll be given a start date and onboarded into your first stage. We keep cohorts small deliberately — this isn't a course you disappear into.

How much money do I need to start? ▼

The goal of the MarketEye journey is to get you trading with capital from our funding partners — not your own. You'll need to cover the cost of your tools and platform access during the Learn and Prove stages, but you're not expected to fund a trading account out of pocket to reach that point.

Is this for complete beginners? ▼

Yes. We build from the ground up — chart reading, entry and exit logic, risk management, and the psychology behind consistent trading. No prior knowledge assumed. In fact, beginners often progress faster because they haven't built bad habits yet.

How much time does this take? ▼

The mentorship requires a real commitment. You'll need roughly 90 minutes to 2 hours on any day you trade, plus time for daily homework and review. Most members trade before work or during a set morning window, some members trade evening and overnight, depending on their current schedules. It's flexible in when you do it — but not in whether you do it.

What if I lose money? ▼

Losses are part of trading — every professional trader has them. What separates consistent traders is how they manage them. The entire MarketEye framework is built around risk control first: you define your risk before every trade, and you never let a single loss derail the plan. You'll also be trading simulated capital during the Prove stage, so you're not risking real money until you've demonstrated you're ready.

What if I've tried trading before and it didn't work? ▼

Most traders who struggle are missing one of three things: a genuine edge, proper risk management, or the right framework to follow consistently. The workshop will help you identify which one it was. Many people who come through MarketEye have traded before — and find that the structure is what was missing, not the ability.
