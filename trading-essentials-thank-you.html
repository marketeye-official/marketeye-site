The programme

What Trading Essentials covers

The programme is structured around the fundamentals that separate consistent traders from everyone else — not tips, not signals, not shortcuts.

📐
Price Action & Market Structure
How to read a chart without relying on indicators. You'll learn to identify support, resistance, trend, and breakdowns directly from price — the foundation of every other skill in the programme.
🛡️
Risk Management & the Pre-Trade Checklist
The single biggest differentiator between traders who last and those who blow up. Every member gets the MarketEye pre-trade checklist — a simple decision framework that removes guesswork before you ever place a trade.
📈
Going Long — Profiting from Rising Markets
The strategy most beginners start with. You'll learn to identify high-probability long setups, set entries with logic, and manage the trade from open to close — including where and why to exit.
📉
Going Short — Profiting from Falling Markets
Most traders only know how to profit when prices rise. Going short is one of the most powerful and misunderstood tools in trading — we demonstrate both directions live, using real markets and real setups.
🧠
Trader Psychology & Discipline
Why intelligent people blow up accounts, and what consistent traders do differently. Psychology is not separate from strategy — it's built into how we approach every session and every trade.
📊
Markets, Instruments & Timeframes
Futures, forex, indices, commodities. How to choose what to trade, when to trade it, and on what timeframe — matched to your schedule, your risk tolerance, and your goals.

The MarketEye way

Right strategy. Real process.

Three ideas that underpin everything we do. Not slogans — actual principles that show up in every session, every trade, every conversation in the community.

1
Process beats prediction
Nobody can predict markets. Consistent traders don't try to. Instead they follow a repeatable process — one that stacks the odds in their favour over many trades, not just one.
2
Small losses. Medium wins.
The math of successful trading is simple: keep losses small, let winners run. The pre-trade checklist and risk management framework is built entirely around this one principle.
3
You learn by doing
No amount of reading replaces being in the room during a live session. The members who improve fastest are the ones who show up, ask questions, and stay active in the community — not the ones who watch recordings in silence.
Common questions

Things people ask early on

Honest answers. No fluff.

Do I need prior trading experience?
No. Trading Essentials is built to work for complete beginners. You'll hear terms you don't recognise in the first session — write them down and ask in Slack. Every experienced member in the community was a beginner once.
What markets does MarketEye trade?
Primarily futures (ES, NQ, GC, CL), forex, and indices. Sessions cover the most active markets at the time. You're not locked into any single instrument.
How much time do I need to commit each week?
Minimum two to three hours per week — one live session plus follow-up in Slack and ClickUp. More time = faster progress. Many members carve out 30 minutes per day.
What happens if I miss a session?
Every session is recorded and uploaded to ClickUp within 24 hours. You won't fall behind. Attend live whenever possible — but the recordings are full-quality and include all Q&A.
