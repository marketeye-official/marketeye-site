About This Agreement

The Community is an educational platform for the exchange of knowledge, strategies, and discussions related to futures, forex, stocks, options, and cryptocurrency trading. The Community does not provide investment, financial, legal, or tax advice of any kind.

Code of Conduct

All Members agree to engage respectfully and professionally. The following are grounds for immediate membership revocation:

  • They may lose some or all of their capital.
  • Only risk capital — money that can be lost without jeopardizing financial security or lifestyle — should be used for trading.

Non-Solicitation

During their membership, no Member may solicit, divert, or attempt to divert any other Community Member or Mentor for the purpose of:

General business activity not specifically targeted at Community Members — such as publishing publicly, posting on social media, or teaching in unrelated industries — is not a violation of this section.
