## What to Expect

We'll cover topics like:

- **Market structure** — how to read price action and identify where institutional money is moving
- **Strategy frameworks** — systematic approaches that remove guesswork from your trading
- **Real process** — the routines, habits, and decision filters that separate consistent traders from the rest

## Why Process Beats Prediction

Most traders spend their time trying to predict the next move. The problem? Markets are designed to punish that approach.

> "The goal is not to predict the market. The goal is to have a process that keeps you on the right side of it."

A well-defined process means you're never reacting to noise — you're executing a plan with clear rules for entry, exit, and risk.
