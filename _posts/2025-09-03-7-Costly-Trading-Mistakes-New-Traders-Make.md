# 7 Costly Trading Mistakes New Traders Make

Every trader makes mistakes.

The goal is not to eliminate mistakes entirely.

The goal is to avoid the mistakes that cause the most damage.

## 1. Trading Without a Plan

Entering trades without predefined rules often leads to emotional decision-making.

A written plan creates consistency.

## 2. Risking Too Much on One Trade

No single trade should determine the fate of a trading account.

Successful traders focus on preserving capital.

## 3. Chasing Trades

Many beginners feel pressure to participate in every market move.

Patience is often more profitable than activity.

## 4. Overtrading

More trades do not necessarily mean more profits.

Quality typically outweighs quantity.

## 5. Ignoring Risk Management

Risk management is not exciting.

It is also one of the most important skills a trader can develop.

## 6. Looking for Certainty

Markets involve uncertainty.

Successful traders learn to operate effectively despite it.

## 7. Focusing Only on Entries

Many traders obsess over finding the perfect entry while neglecting exits and position management.

A complete trading plan addresses all three.

## Final Thoughts

Most trading setbacks can be traced back to a handful of common mistakes.

Recognizing them early can save considerable time, money, and frustration.
